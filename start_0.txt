Why are we already making multiple files?
Because I don't want you to develop the habit of putting everything inside main.cpp.
A professional project separates responsibilities.

For example:
DNSRecord.cpp
      ↓
DNS record logic

RootServer.cpp
      ↓
Root server logic

DNSResolver.cpp
      ↓
resolution logic
This is called separation of concerns.



But there's an important OOP lesson here
We're going to introduce a new class:
DNSResolver
This class will become the brain of our system.
Its job is not to store every DNS record.
Its job is to coordinate the DNS servers.
That's an important software-engineering principle:
One class should have one clear responsibility.
